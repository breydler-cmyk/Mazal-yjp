About

A community built around business exposure.

MAZAL YJP brings together Jews of all walks of life across Philadelphia and the suburbs — with a focus on connecting members to those already building: founders who started their own companies, and executives leading them.

How it works

01

Monthly speakers

Each month, we host a speaker from the community — someone who's started a business or leads one — for an honest conversation about how they got there.

02

Real networking

Every event is paired with dedicated networking time, so conversations that start in the room don't end when it does.

03

Open to all

MAZAL is for Jews of all backgrounds and stages of their career — the common thread is showing up for the community.

Run by Ben Reydler

MAZAL YJP is organized and hosted by Ben Reydler, based in Philadelphia and the suburbs. Reach out any time — details on the contact page.
